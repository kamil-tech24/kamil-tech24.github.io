Featured case study

End-to-end recruitment automation in GoHighLevel

Staffing & hiring · client kept confidential

The challenge

A hiring process runs on sessions: group interviews, orientation, then training. Tracking who showed up, who completed their forms, and who should move on, by hand, across every stage and every recurring session, is slow and easy to get wrong.

What I built

A complete candidate journey inside GoHighLevel, modelled like an applicant tracking system. Candidates move through an ATS-style group interview stage, an orientation stage, and a two-part recurring training program, and the system decides on its own who advances and who drops out.

  • Custom contact fields track attendance and form completion at every stage.
  • Automatic stage moves: candidates advance when they complete the required steps, or move to Disqualified when they don't.
  • Scheduled status checks run a few hours after each session, so decisions happen on time without anyone remembering to check.
  • Recurring two-part training handled end to end, session after session.
